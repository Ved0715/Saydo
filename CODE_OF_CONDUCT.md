# Code of conduct

Saydo follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be kind and patient, assume good faith, give and take feedback on the work rather than the person, and make room for people new to the project. Harassment and personal attacks are not tolerated in any project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and chat.

Report a problem to the maintainers privately (see SECURITY.md for the contact). Reports are kept confidential and answered within a few days.
